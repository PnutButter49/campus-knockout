TRAINING ROOM

HOW TO PLAY

READ THE TELL

Every campaign opponent telegraphs attacks. Watch the warning and learn the pattern.

DODGE & COUNTER

Dodge just before impact. A successful dodge opens a counter window for extra damage.

MANAGE STAMINA

Punches, blocking, and online dodges use stamina. Button-mashing leaves you exhausted.

ONLINE FIGHT

Create a room, share the six-character code, both fighters ready up, and the host starts a live 1v1 match.

ONLINE ROUNDS

Online matches run up to three 45-second rounds. A KO ends it immediately; otherwise rounds are judged by remaining health.

BEAT THE SEMESTER

In the campaign, defeat all five opponents. Score rewards accuracy, health, speed, counters and difficulty.
